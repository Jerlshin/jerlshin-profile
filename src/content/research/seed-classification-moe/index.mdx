## Scope

Fine-grained visual classification asks a model to separate categories that differ by small,
localised details rather than by overall shape or context. Seed cultivars are a demanding instance
of the problem: the discriminative evidence is texture and margin detail at a scale where intra-class
variation from lighting and orientation is comparable to the inter-class variation being measured.

This work approaches that setting with two components:

- **Self-supervised pretraining**, so representation learning does not depend on the size of the
  labelled cultivar set.
- **A mixture-of-experts classification head**, so capacity is allocated per-region of the label
  space instead of being shared uniformly across visually dissimilar groups.
