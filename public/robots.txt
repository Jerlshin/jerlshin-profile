# robots.txt — served verbatim from public/.
#
# The whole site is meant to be indexed; that is a large part of why it stopped
# being a single-page React app (audit finding #7). So the policy is "allow
# everything", and the only job left is pointing crawlers at the sitemap.
#
# /og/ is disallowed rather than left open: those PNGs are social-card assets
# referenced by og:image, not pages. A crawler that indexes them adds nine
# images to Google Images that nobody searched for and that link nowhere useful.
# Social scrapers (Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Slack) fetch og:image directly and do
# not consult robots.txt, so this does not affect link previews.
#
# /search is disallowed for the same reason its <head> carries noindex: its
# content is a search box, and every page it can find is indexed on its own.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /og/
Disallow: /search
Disallow: /styleguide
